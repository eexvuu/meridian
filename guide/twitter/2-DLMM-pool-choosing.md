When we want to DLMM using
@MeteoraAG
, the first thing after choosing the coin would be choosing which DLMM pool to use. Getting the right pool to DLMM is crucial as being in range will keep the fees printed and getting out of range will make us have bigger impermanent loss (IL).

So I will detail about my simple method on to which pool to use, rather than just going with the highest TVL pool, for me it is better to gauge the correction size correctly to maximise fees earned during a dump.

First you have to understand that there are many strategies that we can use to earn fees from DLMM. Some people prefer to earn on big volume churn. My method is the old classic "let me eat your dump and give me fees, then sell on a pump to earn profits." That's why I need to predict how big a correction could be to choose the pool size.

You will be looking at charts and using some simple charting tools and one indicator.

1/7 🧵

I will use lmeow chart on 15mins as an example for the part before the big pump. These are the steps I always do on a new chart.

1. Go to the chart and add indicator, find the supertrend indicator and add it. (Picture 1)
2. Next on the left side choose the Fib retracement tool. (Picture 2)
3. Choose the highest point and drag it anywhere, then double click on the Fib to edit.
4. For the style, edit 4 of them to contain 0.51, 0.43, 0.27, 0.19. (Picture 3)
5. Then go to coordinates and change the bottom price to 0. (Picture 4)

Then we are done, next how to use it.

2/7

If you have done everything correctly, it would look like the picture below.

Next the numbers on the Fib.

0.51 - 100bins one sided sol is 49%
0.43 - 125bins one sided sol is 57%
0.27 - 200bins one sided sol is 73%
0.19 - 250bins one sided sol is 81%

1 - 0.49 = 0.51. Hence how we get the numbers.

So next on how to use with the Supertrend indicator.

3/7

From here we can see where the ranges are. We can see that the supertrend support is under the 125bins range but above the 200bins range. So I would choose the nearest pool range below the supertrend support. (200 bins)

As price reaches support it tends to bounce, but as we know it does not always happen and price tends to breakdown as we are dealing with volatile meme coins.
But it gives us a good support area to make decisions from. So naturally I would choose the bin range below the supertrend support which is the 200bins.

That is how I choose my pool, as for why I chose 200/10% is because I feel that the coin is still new and I like using the 10% fee pool for new coins.

4/7

Things to note

1. Always draw the fib retracement from the highest point because we need the volume to cover IL and volume is highest when price is breaking all time highs usually.
2. This is a retracement play, we are providing liquidity for people to dump into and we earn fees.
3. You have to choose the right coin to do this strategy with. (Refer to my previous long post about how to choose coins)
4. With more experience and luck, you might just be able to repeat profitable plays like Lmeow.
