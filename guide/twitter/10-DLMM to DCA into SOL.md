How do you use Meteora DLMM to buy more SOL using the SOL-USDC pool or technically DCA with bonus.

In this example I used the 10/0.1% SOL-USDC pool.

I put in $1168.72 USDC into the pool and opened a standard 70bins position using SPOT.

It has been running for 2 days and let's say if I decided to close it today and convert everything into SOL.

I would have (200.36usdc + 21.39usdc fees)=221.75usdc, at current SOL price of 83.68 that is 2.65sol.

Add that with 11.24sol and 0.258sol fees we get a total of 14.147sol.

Compare that with just straight buying the SOL with all the USDC when the pool was opened at 88.67 which will result in 13.18sol.

That results in 7.33% more SOL obtained as we are buying lower as well as earning from fees.

Hope that helps with explaining why using DLMM to DCA IN is a better alternative that to just buy straight at market price.
